Angel

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Angel is a private, local-first backup and organization utility operated by Jensy Jimenez. This policy explains how Angel handles information when it uses Google Drive.

Information accessed

Angel requests Google Drive access so it can create, read, update, and verify the backup files in its designated backup folder. Angel does not request access to unrelated Google services.

How information is used

Drive access is used only to upload, validate, and restore the operator’s encrypted backup repository. Angel does not sell, rent, advertise against, or share personal information with third parties.

Storage and retention

Backup data is retained in the operator-controlled local storage and Google Drive locations configured for Angel. OAuth credentials are stored in the operator’s local encrypted configuration and macOS Keychain. The operator can revoke access from their Google Account security settings at any time.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Angel’s data practices change. Questions can be sent to jensyjimenez723@gmail.com.
